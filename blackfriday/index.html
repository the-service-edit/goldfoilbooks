For Dani to check

  1. Time zone. The pages say Sydney time, like the last two launches. 12am Friday Sydney is 9pm Thursday in Perth.
  2. Shipping weeks. Five sets a week means sets 16 to 20 ship the week of 21 December, so the page says they may arrive after Christmas. Are those weeks right, alongside single-book orders? Australia Post's Christmas dates still need checking.
  3. Numbered sets. "Every Black Friday set is numbered, 1 to 20, on a card I sign." This is a suggestion, not your copy. Keep it or cut it?
  4. Gift card. The page promises a printable gift card with an artwork on it, emailed the day someone orders. Happy to make one?
  5. Free artwork. The 20 files would be emailed as one download link, not added through the cart. The set page says "the day you order".
  6. Past buyers. Early access is for people on the list who haven't bought yet. Do people who already own books one to three get early access too, or a code to finish their set?
  7. How the 20 sets are split. Squarespace counts stock separately for each size and edge choice (now 5 of each). If 6 people want Large with gold edges, the sixth sees "sold out". Keep 5 each, or move more to Large with gold edges?
  8. Every Black Friday line on these pages is new copy written for the mockup. Change anything that doesn't sound like you.
