A new digital experience, built around how people find venues.

The new CVBS website wasn't designed simply to look more modern. It was rebuilt to make the service easier to understand, venues easier to discover, expertise easier to trust and enquiries easier to make.

Conference Venues & Booking Services · August 2026

Before we look at what changed, let's look at why it needed to change.

The starting point

CVBS had evolved.
The website hadn't evolved with it.

Behind the scenes: thirty-seven years of venue relationships, a trusted team, and clients who keep coming back. The website told very little of that story, and asked a lot of anyone trying to enquire.

Built on an ageing template Quote form supplied as a PDF No venues to explore
conferencevenues.com.au
What was holding it back

Six limitations, seen through a client's eyes.

01 · FIRST IMPRESSION

The scale of CVBS wasn't visible

The homepage opened on a dated template: a stock hero image, one button, and three short paragraphs. Nothing above the fold signalled an established, professional operation.

02 · SERVICE CLARITY

The service took work to understand

What CVBS does, that it's free to the client, and why it beats searching alone was spread across inner pages. A first-time visitor had to assemble the story themselves.

03 · VENUE DISCOVERY

There were no venues to discover

No destinations to browse, no venue pages, no way to move from "I need a venue in Sydney" to seeing what's possible. The site described a service, but never showed the product.

04 · TRUST

The proof was buried

Since 1989, the client list, the testimonials: all real, all persuasive, and almost all sitting on the About page rather than working for CVBS at every step.

05 · CONVERSION

Enquiring meant downloading a PDF

"Get a Quote" led to a downloadable PDF form, with the online version marked coming soon. Every extra step at the exact moment of intent costs enquiries.

06 · MOBILE

Mobile was an afterthought

The layout technically responded, but hierarchy, tap targets and enquiry pathways weren't designed for the device many organisers first reach for.

Six principles behind the rebuild

Every decision traces back to one of these.

CLARITY

Anyone landing on any page should understand what CVBS does, and that it costs them nothing, within seconds.

DISCOVERY

Make finding the right destination and venue feel like exploring, not searching. Give visitors somewhere to go next.

TRUST

Bring 1989, the client list, the testimonials and the real people forward, so credibility is felt before it's needed.

GUIDANCE

Help visitors understand what happens after they enquire, so starting a brief feels safe rather than committal.

CONVERSION

A clear, low-friction next step from every page: tell us about your event, and we do the searching.

MOBILE

Design the experience for the device organisers actually reach for first, not as a shrunken desktop.

The moment that matters most

From a PDF download to a guided brief.

On the previous site, the highest-intent visitors, the ones ready to enquire, were handed a PDF form to download, fill in and send back. The new site meets that same moment with a short guided brief: your event, your venue needs, your details, done.

The thank-you screen then explains exactly what happens next, so the conversation starts with confidence.

Designed for the small screen

Small details, big difference

The changes you feel more than you notice.

CTA language

"Start your brief" and "Tell us about your event" replace "Get a Quote". Enquiring reads as beginning a conversation, not requesting paperwork.

Guidance before commitment

The four-step process appears on nearly every page, so the answer to "what happens next?" is never more than a glance away.

Trust, made ambient

Since 1989, free to you, shortlist in 48 hours: the reassurance strip travels with the visitor instead of living on one page.

Cards you want to touch

Destinations and venues are presented as considered, hoverable cards, with consistent imagery that sells the experience of the event itself.

Offers with substance

Each offer names the venue, the inclusions and the timeframe, then connects straight to an enquiry, so interest never dead-ends.

Real people, named

Contact and About lead with Karen and Anthony rather than a generic inbox. You know who picks up the brief before you send it.
