Drawn by hand

Seven layers, one panel

Every panel is built in Procreate on an iPad. Line work first, then colour, one surface at a time, each on its own layer.

  • Line workBase
  • SkinLayer
  • ClothesLayer
  • HairLayer
  • StoneLayer
  • WoodLayer
  • DecorLayer
  • OutsideLayer

Four panels are drawn this way for every rebind: a front cover, a back cover and two endpaper spreads. Not a filter, not a font, not AI.

Meet the maker

Gold Foil Books started the way most good things do: quietly, in a corner, surrounded by books and sketchbooks. I am a self-taught bookbinder and digital artist based in Wollongong, Australia, working from a home studio.

Every illustration is hand-drawn in Procreate, and every rebind is crafted by hand with care, using premium materials and meticulous attention to detail. I believe the stories we love deserve to be celebrated.

Thank you for being here. I hope you find something that brings a little more magic to your bookshelf or home.
