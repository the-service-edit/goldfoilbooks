The maker

One pair of hands, start to finish.

I couldn’t find the edition that I wanted, so I taught myself how to make it.

I am a self-taught bookbinder and digital artist in Wollongong, working from a home studio. The artwork came before the binding. I learned to bind because the drawings I had made deserved a better object than a paperback.

Every panel is drawn in Procreate on an iPad. I print it, foil it, edge it and bind the book myself. Nothing is licensed in, nothing is outsourced, and nothing is generated.

A rebind starts life as a paperback and the text block is never altered. Everything around it changes: a foiled front, spine and back, illustrated endpapers at both ends, metallic gold page edges, and a premium laminated finish. Finished size is roughly 13.5cm wide by 20.5cm tall. The edges are painted by hand on books one and two, and sprayed on book three.
