About

I taught myself to make the edition I wanted.

Chapter 54 · back endpaper for A Court of Mist and Fury

Gold Foil Books started the way most good things do: quietly, in a corner, surrounded by books and sketchbooks.

I am a self-taught bookbinder and digital artist based in Wollongong, Australia, working from a home studio. Every illustration is hand-drawn in Procreate, and every rebind is crafted by hand with care, using premium materials and meticulous attention to detail.

I believe the stories we love deserve to be celebrated, and that a book you have read five times should look like it.

Thank you for being here. I hope you find something that brings a little more magic to your bookshelf or home.
