Common questions

I cannot find my artwork download

Check the email address you used at checkout, including the junk folder. The download link is sent straight after payment.

If you created an account with that same email, the file is also waiting in your account. If you checked out as a guest, message me with your order number and I will send it again.

I ordered but never made an account

That is fine, and your order is not affected. Create an account using the same email address you used at checkout and your order history will be there.

I have forgotten my password

Use Forgot password on the sign in screen. The reset email comes from Squarespace, so check your junk folder if it has not arrived within a few minutes.

Where is my book up to?

Each book is made to order and the current processing time is approximately 2 to 4 weeks depending on order volume. You will get a dispatch email with tracking when yours is on its way.
