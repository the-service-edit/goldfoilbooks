Five hand-drawn panels. Starfall on the cover, a snowbound cabin on the back, and the wedding and the kitchen inside.

Front cover

Starfall in Velaris

Feyre and Rhysand on the balcony, foreheads together, the sky above Velaris coming apart into light. Framed in wisteria and drawn at 2829 by 4000, the largest of the panels, because the sky needed the room.

Back cover

The Cabin

The cabin in the snow with its windows lit, under the peaks. It carries the foiled line on the finished edition, which is why the composition leaves the upper third open: that space is where the gold goes.
