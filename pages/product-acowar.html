About the binding

These editions begin as standard paperbacks and are slightly enlarged during the rebinding process. The original text block and pages remain unaltered, resulting in a finished book that is smaller than a standard hardcover.

This is a handmade item. Minor variations may occur between copies, making each book unique.

Inside the front board

The sun was shining when I left you.

The front endpaper runs the full width of the opening: a crowned court on a terrace above a lily pond, with the line foiled across the water.

The edges

Sprayed, not painted.

The edges on this edition are sprayed metallic gold. The other two in the set are painted on by hand.

Sprayed edges lay down more evenly and catch the light in a flatter, brighter sheet. Hand-painted edges vary slightly along the block. Neither is the better method; they are different finishes, and it seemed worth telling you which one you are buying.
