Drawn by hand

Inspired by scenes that stay with me

Every piece of artwork begins as I’m reading the book and a scene captures my attention. I sketch out the idea in Procreate on my iPad, starting with the initial composition and line work before gradually building up the details.

Once the line work is complete, I bring the illustration to life with colour, layering in shading, highlights and finer details until the final artwork takes shape.

The process is intentionally detailed, with each illustration taking an average of 30 hours to complete. Every finished illustration is an original work created specifically for each edition, making each design a personal part of the book.

From the very first sketch to the final details, every line and layer is drawn by me.

Meet the maker

Gold Foil Books started in 2025 with a fabric-covered rebind of ACOTAR. Fabric covers were the obvious starting point, but I wanted to bring my own art into the book, not just wrap it. So I started illustrating the covers myself, drawing the characters and scenes straight from the story I was binding. I’m Dani, a self-taught bookbinder and digital artist based in Wollongong, Australia, working from a home studio.

Every illustration is hand-drawn in Procreate, and every rebind is crafted by hand with care, using premium materials and meticulous attention to detail. I believe the stories we love deserve to be celebrated.

Thank you for being here. I hope you find something that brings a little more magic to your bookshelf or home.
